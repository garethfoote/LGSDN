Start with the people using the service

This prototype explores a featured image as a full-bleed backdrop for the article title, lead, and service area. The article body begins on a calm canvas below, so the colour and image treatment stays focused on the introduction.

What this experiment is testing

  • A service colour wash over the image helps the white title and lead stay readable.
  • The service area sits in a compact, solid canvas panel so its label and icon remain legible.
  • The content stays in a single reading order; the composition shifts from split to stacked as space narrows.
