Typography

Srbija Sans is used sparingly for titles and headings. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next carries paragraph, lead, and interface copy. The system documents typography in two layers: semantic heading levels describe content structure, while named roles and selectors describe visual treatment.

Semantic heading levels

Choose H1–H6 from the hierarchy of the content, not from the size you want on screen. A heading level can take a different visual role in a different component, and a visual role can be used by more than one heading level.
